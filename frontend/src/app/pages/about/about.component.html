The Publication

About Travel Bharat.

A new-age onboard reading experience — crafted for the way modern India travels.

Travel Bharat is a premium onboard lifestyle and travel magazine designed exclusively for passengers of India's flagship semi high-speed trains.

Curated for today's discerning traveller, the magazine blends travel, culture, business, hospitality, technology, automobiles, wellness, and luxury lifestyle into one engaging reading experience.

More than just a magazine, Travel Bharat is a platform that celebrates the spirit of modern India — its destinations, innovation, people, and stories — while enhancing the journey experience for rail passengers across the country.

Our Vision

A publication that moves with the country.

To redefine onboard engagement by creating a sophisticated, informative, and visually rich publication that reflects the aspirations of contemporary India and the evolving experience of rail travel.

Every journey should inspire, inform, and connect the traveller to the stories of the country moving past their window.

Distribution & Presence

Where we're read.

Travel Bharat is strategically distributed onboard premium train services, ensuring direct access to a focused and high-value audience throughout their journey.

Positioned within a distraction-free travel environment, the magazine enjoys extended reader attention and strong engagement.

With expansion plans across major premium rail corridors, Travel Bharat is envisioned as one of India's most influential onboard media platforms.
