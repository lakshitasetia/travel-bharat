The journey begins

Stories for the way modern India travels.

Our journey begins with the New Delhi (NDLS) - Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra (SMVDK) Vande Bharat Express, one of the country's most important premium rail corridors connecting the national capital with the gateway to the revered Vaishno Devi pilgrimage.

Through a carefully curated mix of travel, tourism, culture, heritage, business, lifestyle, automobiles, technology, wellness, food, inspirational stories, kids and youth stories, Travel Bharat brings an engaging reading experience directly to passengers during their journey.

The New Delhi-Katra Vande Bharat service operates as Train Nos. 22477/22478, covering approximately 655 km between New Delhi and Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra.

A captive audience

A premium environment for meaningful attention.

Unlike conventional print media, Travel Bharat reaches its audience at a unique moment - during their journey. Passengers are seated comfortably for several hours, travelling between major cities and pilgrimage, tourism and business destinations.

The New Delhi-Katra journey takes approximately 8 hours 20 minutes on the current schedule, giving the onboard publication substantial journey-time visibility.

Beyondcirculation

From circulation to readership

A magazine copy can be read by more than one passenger. Most passengers take the magazine with them even after the journey, allowing readership to multiply as copies are shared, browsed and passed among fellow travellers.

With every passenger getting a copy every day, Travel Bharat has the potential to generate readership running into lakhs over a sustained monthly and annual cycle.

This makes Travel Bharat more than a conventional print publication - it is a high-frequency, high-visibility onboard communication platform.

The New Delhi-Katra connect

One route. Multiple markets.

The route connects New Delhi with Ambala, Ludhiana, Kathua, Jammu Tawi, Martyr Captain Tushar Mahajan (Udhampur) and Shri Mata Vaishno Devi Katra and vice versa.

It brings together multiple passenger segments and geographic markets within a single premium travel ecosystem. For brands seeking visibility across Delhi NCR, Haryana, Punjab and Jammu & Kashmir, Travel Bharat provides a highly relevant communication platform.
